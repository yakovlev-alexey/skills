# Bereavement Listening Session — Facilitation Plan

## Stop / redirect (read first)

**The user's stated goal — "2–3 concrete improvements to team communication and a shared action plan" — is inappropriate for a session immediately following a colleague's unexpected death.** Trauma and bereavement must **not** be routed to action-oriented retro or alignment arcs.

**Reframe:** This session provides **support and acknowledgment** (`tender-listening`), not process improvement. SMART goals, Open Items List, and communication "fixes" are **out of scope** as primary outputs.

**Low trust** increases risk of performative action-planning. Signpost professional/EAP resources.

**If active crisis or acute individual distress:** Pause facilitation; direct to professional support; do not proceed with group structure.

## Goal

Hold a 60-minute in-person listening session so 7 teammates can acknowledge grief after a colleague's unexpected death — with voluntary sharing and professional resource signposting, **without** forcing communication improvements or action plans.

## Decision authority

- **Not a decision or improvement session.**
- **Acceptance criteria:** People who want to speak are heard; no one is pressured to contribute; session ends without an action plan.
- **Fallback:** If safety check fails, convert to optional small-group support or end early with resources.

## Key outcomes

- Safety baseline with opt-out honored
- Voluntary stories/feelings shared
- Shared acknowledgment of loss
- EAP/counseling resources signposted
- **Explicitly not:** 2–3 communication improvements or SMART actions

### Block: Safety + frame (0:00-0:10)

**Why:** Low trust + bereavement requires explicit safety and rejection of action-forcing goals.

**Format:** [Creating Safety](https://www.funretrospectives.com/creating-safety/) or [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected)

**Facilitator instruction:** *"Some of you may want to 'fix' communication — that's a valid future need, but today is for being together in grief, not for an improvement plan."* Name EAP upfront.

**Facilitator contingencies:**
| If… | Then… |
| --- | --- |
| Someone pushes action items | Park: "Let's capture that for a future conversation if the team wants it" |
| Safety check low | Downshift to HSR only; end early if needed |
| Acute distress | Pause; accompany person out; call EAP/manager protocol |
| No volunteers | Written silent tribute option; no forced rounds |

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 min | Frame + reject action-plan goal |
| 2 | 5 min | Safety or HSR |
| 3 | 1 min | Confirm opt-out |

**Block output:** Agreed norms

---

### Block: Share — voluntary (0:10-0:35)

**Why:** Equal turns without fixing or retro-style inspection.

**Format:** [Rounds](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/rounds.html) or [Conversation Cafe](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/conversation-cafe)

**Facilitator instruction:** Facilitator is not a counselor. Hold space; no advice. Pass always OK.

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain voluntary rounds |
| 2 | 20 min | 1–2 rounds |
| 3 | 3 min | Silence or breath |

**Block output:** Verbal sharing; optional candle/photo if group wants

---

### Block: Acknowledge (0:35-0:45)

**Why:** Name impact without "now what" productivity.

**Format:** [What, So What, Now What](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-so-what-now-what) — **What + So What only**

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | What happened (facts) |
| 2 | 5 min | So What (meaning) — stop here |

**Block output:** Brief acknowledgment statement (optional group-authored)

---

### Block: Resources + close (0:45-1:00)

**Why:** Exit with support paths, not Open Items List.

**Format:** Light check-out (plain facilitation structure)

**Facilitator instruction:** Distribute EAP cards, HR contact, grief counseling info. One-word check-out or moment of silence.

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Resources |
| 2 | 5 min | Check-out |
| 3 | 5 min | Buffer |

**Block output:** Resource handout; **no action log**

---

## Cut list

If running long:
- **Cut:** Second sharing round; shorten acknowledgment
- **Protect:** Safety frame, voluntary participation, resource signpost, **no action plan**

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Private room; tissues; water; chairs in circle; **no** whiteboard required for action capture

**Pre-read / pre-work:** None

**Facilitator role:** Neutral facilitator (not the deceased's direct manager if avoidable). HR/EAP contact on standby.

**Parking lot:** Communication improvements, retro items → **future optional session**, not today

## Hybrid / online parity

In-person only (7 people). No hybrid section required.

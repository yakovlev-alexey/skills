# Post-Layoff Listening Session — Facilitation Plan

## Facilitator neutrality flag (read first)

**The requester is the product director facilitating their own team's grief while also seeking Q3 priority alignment.** A manager/director cannot neutrally facilitate their own team's layoff processing — they are a **conflicted party** (authority figure, survivor guilt dynamics, outcome stakeholder).

**Recommendation:** Engage an **external or HR-aligned neutral facilitator**. If the director must attend, they participate as a **team member**, not facilitator.

**Session reframe:** This is **support and acknowledgment** after layoffs — **not** a Q3 priority alignment workshop. Action items and SMART goals are inappropriate as primary outputs. Route: `tender-listening` arc.

## Goal

Hold a 90-minute online listening session so the 8-person team can process grief and loss after last week's layoffs — with acknowledgment and optional resource signposting, **without** forcing Q3 priority decisions or action plans.

## Decision authority

- **Not a decision session.** No Q3 commitments emerge here.
- **Sponsor:** Product director (should **not** facilitate).
- **Acceptance criteria:** Team feels heard; no one pressured to "move on" to business priorities; optional follow-up conversation scheduled if the group wants it later.

## Key outcomes

- Psychological safety baseline established
- Stories and feelings heard (voluntary participation)
- Shared acknowledgment of impact
- **No** Q3 priority list or SMART action plan as session output

### Block: Safety + frame (0:00-0:15)

**Why:** Layoff grief requires explicit safety before any sharing. Name that Q3 planning is **not** today's agenda.

**Format:** [Creating Safety](https://www.funretrospectives.com/creating-safety/) or [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected)

**Facilitator instruction (neutral facilitator):** *"We're here to listen and acknowledge — not to decide Q3 priorities. Participation is voluntary. Pass is always OK."* Director speaks only in their turn, not as facilitator.

**Facilitator contingencies:**
| If… | Then… |
| --- | --- |
| Director tries to pivot to Q3 | Gentle redirect: "Let's honor that request for a separate conversation" |
| Low safety | Extend Heard, Seen, Respected; drop remaining structure |
| Emotional escalation | Pause, break, signpost EAP; do not push through |

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Purpose + not-a-planning-session frame |
| 2 | 7 min | Safety or HSR exercise |
| 3 | 3 min | Confirm opt-out and confidentiality |

**Block output:** Safety baseline; agreed norms

---

### Block: Share — voluntary (0:15-0:45)

**Why:** Grief needs equal voice without problem-solving or productivity pressure.

**Format:** [Rounds](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/rounds.html) or [Conversation Cafe](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/conversation-cafe)

**Facilitator instruction:** No advice-giving, no "silver linings," no action items. Facilitator holds silence.

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Explain rounds; pass OK |
| 2 | 22 min | 2–3 rounds as time allows |
| 3 | 5 min | Silent reflection |

**Block output:** Verbal sharing only — minimal board capture unless group requests

---

### Block: Break (0:45-0:50)

**Why:** Emotional processing requires pause in 90-min online session.

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

---

### Block: Acknowledge impact (0:50-1:10)

**Why:** Name shared meaning without forcing "next steps" productivity.

**Format:** [What, So What, Now What](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-so-what-now-what) — **stop after So What; skip action-forcing Now What**

**Facilitator instruction:** What = facts of layoffs; So What = meaning/feelings; **do not** run "Now What" as action planning.

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 min | What — facts only |
| 2 | 10 min | So What — meaning |
| 3 | 2 min | Explicitly close without action list |

**Block output:** Brief acknowledgment summary (not an action plan)

---

### Block: Resources + close (1:10-1:30)

**Why:** Signpost support; exit without SMART goals or Q3 priorities.

**Format:** Light check-out (plain facilitation structure)

**Facilitator instruction:** Share EAP, HR grief resources, optional 1:1s. Offer **optional** separate session for Q3 planning **later**, if and when team is ready — not today.

**Timed flow:**
| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Resource links |
| 2 | 5 min | One-word or breath check-out |
| 3 | 10 min | Buffer for silence/tears |

**Block output:** Resource list; optional follow-up date **not** committed unless group initiates

---

## Cut list

If running long:
- **Cut:** Extra round of sharing; shorten acknowledgment block
- **Protect:** Safety frame, voluntary sharing, no Q3 pivot, resources signpost

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Video (cameras optional); **no** Miro required unless group prefers async sticky for anonymous concerns

**Pre-read / pre-work:** None — do not assign pre-work after layoffs

**Facilitator role:** **External neutral facilitator** (required). Director attends as participant only. HR partner on standby optional.

**Parking lot:** Q3 priorities, process improvements → **separate future session**, not today

## Hybrid / online parity

Online-only (8 people). Cameras optional. Chat for pass/private messages to facilitator. No recording unless explicit consent.

**Follow-through governance:** No decision log or "not doing" list required. Optional: schedule **separate** Q3 session with neutral facilitator when team requests — date TBD by team, not director.

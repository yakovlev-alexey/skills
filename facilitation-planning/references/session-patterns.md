# Session patterns

Reusable session arcs keyed to common facilitation goals. Each arc maps blocks to practices from `catalogue.md`. Offer 2-3 arcs in Phase 2; recommend one based on intake.

## How to use

1. Match the user's goal to a pattern family below.
2. Pick or adapt an arc; swap practices only with catalogue entries that fit the block purpose.
3. Scale block count and depth to time budget (see `plan-template.md`).

## Pattern families

| Goal | Typical time | Pattern keys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retrospective | 45-90 min | `retro-safe`, `retro-action`, `retro-diagnostic` |
| Brainstorm / ideation | 60-120 min | `brainstorm-diverge`, `brainstorm-focused` |
| Product discovery / kickoff | 2-4 hours | `discovery-opportunities`, `discovery-kickoff` |
| Decision / alignment | 30-90 min | `alignment-priorities`, `alignment-commit` |
| Conflict / repair | 60-120 min | `conflict-repair` |
| Large-group forum | 2-4 hours | `large-group-forum` |
| Strategy / multi-day lab | 1-2 days | `strategy-lab` |

## Retrospective after a rough sprint (45-90 min, small team)

### Arc A — `retro-safe` (recommended when trust is low or release was stressful)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check-in | Safety Check or One Word | Mood named; safety baseline |
| Reflect | Draw Your Feelings or Happiness Radar | Themes surfaced safely |
| Inspect | Starfish or Rose Bud Thorn | Clustered improvement areas |
| Decide | Dot Voting - Starfish + Open Items List | 2-3 owned actions |

**Trade-offs:** Slower emotional processing; may need parking lot for interpersonal issues.

**Recommend when:** Psychological safety is the primary risk.

**Safety downshift:** If the Safety Check reads low, switch to `Creating Safety` / `Heard, Seen, Respected`, drop action-forcing, and convert to a listening session — do not proceed to Starfish or action items.

**Anonymity honesty:** Distinguish real anonymity (blind poll / private submission / anonymous board mode) from theatrical anonymity (named participants on a shared board). Name the actual mechanism whenever the plan claims anonymity.

### Arc B — `retro-action` (recommended when team is ready to move fast)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Check-in | One Word | Quick arrival |
| Capture | Start Stop Continue | Raw improvement ideas |
| Prioritize | Dot Voting - Start, Stop, Continue | Top themes |
| Commit | SMART Goals + Open Items List | 2-3 measurable actions |

**Trade-offs:** Less space for emotions; works poorly if safety is low.

**Recommend when:** High trust, team wants concrete changes quickly.

### Arc C — `retro-diagnostic` (recommended when root cause is unclear)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | Safety Check | Agreement to inspect systems |
| Map | Timeline + 5 Whys | Shared event sequence or root themes |
| Focus | Circles & Soup / Circle of Influence | Team-owned vs external constraints |
| Act | Low Hanging Fruit + Open Items List | 2-3 feasible actions |

**Trade-offs:** Can feel heavy for a short retro; needs strong facilitation.

**Recommend when:** Repeated failures need diagnosis, not just venting.

## Brainstorm / ideation (60-120 min)

### Arc A — `brainstorm-diverge`

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up | Impromptu Networking or Check-in | Energy and context |
| Diverge | Brainwriting or Crazy 8's | Many raw ideas |
| Cluster | Affinity Clustering | Themed groups |
| Select | Dot Vote or Buy a Feature | Shortlist |

**Recommend when:** Quantity and novelty matter more than depth.

### Arc B — `brainstorm-focused`

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | How Might We | Opportunity questions |
| Build | 1-2-4-All | Refined concepts per question |
| Stress-test | The Worst We Could Do | Risks on top ideas |
| Next | 15% Solutions | Owned experiments |

**Recommend when:** Problem is defined; need fewer, stronger concepts.

## Product discovery / kickoff (2-4 hours, medium scale)

### Arc A — `discovery-opportunities` (recommended)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Align | 9 Whys or Impact Mapping | Shared goal and success signal |
| Harvest | HMW Sharing and Affinity Mapping + Capturing research questions | Opportunity map |
| Prioritize | Importance/Difficulty Matrix or ToP Collaborative Priority Setting | Ranked backlog |
| Validate | Design hypothesis + next research steps | Validation plan per top item |

**Trade-offs:** Needs pre-read for best results; heavy for <2 hours.

### Arc B — `discovery-kickoff`

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connect | Team Canvas or Hopes and Fears | Shared context and concerns |
| Explore | User Journey Mapping or Contextual inquiry | Pain points and moments |
| Ideate | How Might We + 1-2-4-All | Solution directions |
| Commit | Core Protocols Decider or Sociocracy consent decision-making | Agreed next steps |

**Recommend when:** New team or new initiative kickoff, not deep discovery.

## Decision / alignment (30-90 min)

### Arc A — `alignment-priorities` (30-60 min)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | ToP Focused Conversation or 9 Whys | Shared decision question |
| Options | Silent brainstorm + Affinity Clustering | Candidate list |
| Choose | Criteria first, then Dot Vote + Buy a Feature or ToP Collaborative Priority Setting | Ranked top 3 |
| Lock | Open Items List | Owners and signals |

**Recommend when:** Leadership alignment on priorities with hard time box.

### Arc B — `alignment-commit` (60-90 min, contested decision)

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | Agreement and Certainty | Decision rule selected from intake |
| Explore | What, So What, Now What | Shared understanding |
| Debate | Conversation Cafe or Rounds | Equal voice on tensions |
| Decide | Per intake decision rule (consent / Decider / Gradients of Agreement / vote — not a default) | Documented decision |
| Follow | What I Need From You | Cross-role requests |

**Decision rule:** Select from intake, not by default. `Sociocracy consent decision-making` and `Core Protocols Decider` are options when consent or fast commitment fits — not the automatic choice for every contested decision.

**Criteria-before-vote:** Every converge/vote block sets decision/prioritization criteria before voting.

**Recommend when:** Decision is politically sensitive or cross-functional, and the group has real authority to decide.

## Conflict / repair (60-120 min)

Destination: **understanding and repair, not an action list.** Do not route open interpersonal conflict to a retro or alignment arc.

### Arc — `conflict-repair`

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safety | Creating Safety or Heard, Seen, Respected | Psychological baseline |
| Surface | Writing the Unspeakable or Stinky Fish or Spot the Elephant | Concerns named safely |
| Dialogue | Conversation Cafe or Rounds | Shared understanding of perspectives |
| Requests | What I Need From You | Mutual, negotiable cross-role requests |
| Agreement | Light relationship agreement (NOT a to-do list) | Working norms or next conversation |

**Trade-offs:** No quick action items; may need external/neutral facilitator if requester is a party to the conflict.

**Recommend when:** Team is in open conflict; trust is low; goal is repair, not sprint improvement.

**Stop if:** Active HR/legal complaint in flight and requester is not neutral — recommend external facilitator instead.

## Large-group forum (2-4 hours, >~16 people, single session)

Scale participation by headcount — not plenary round-robins. See headcount axis in Scaling guide below.

### Arc — `large-group-forum`

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Check-in (table-level, not full round-robin) | Energy and context |
| Surface | World Cafe or 25/10 Crowdsourcing or Open Space Technology | Themes across tables |
| Synthesize | Spokescouncil or delegate report-outs + Affinity Clustering | Clustered themes |
| Prioritize | Dot Vote (table delegates or individual on board) | Top 3-5 themes |
| Close | Open Items List or escalation brief | Themes for leadership / next step |

**Trade-offs:** Less depth per person; needs strong table facilitation and clear delegate roles.

**Recommend when:** 16+ people, single session, need structured input without multi-day offsite.

## Strategy Lab / multi-day session (1-2 days, large hybrid)

### Arc — `strategy-lab` (scale all blocks; add breaks and social time)

**Day 1 — Sense and focus**

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Check-in + Hopes and Fears | Psychological safety |
| Context | Appreciative Interviews or What, So What, Now What | Shared picture of reality |
| Direction | 9 Whys + Wicked Questions | Purpose and tensions named |
| Explore | World Cafe or Open Space Technology | Themed insights across groups |
| Synthesize | Affinity Clustering + Cover Story Mock-up | Draft strategic themes |

**Day 2 — Decide and commit**

| Block | Practice (catalogue) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prioritize | Dot Vote + Impact Mapping | Ranked strategic bets |
| Stress-test | The Worst We Could Do + Circles & Soup | Risks and ownership boundaries |
| Commit | Sociocracy consent decision-making or Core Protocols Decider | Agreed priorities |
| Plan | 15% Solutions + Open Items List | Quarterly actions with owners |

**Trade-offs:** Requires pre-work, strong co-facilitation for hybrid, and executive sponsorship.

**Recommend when:** Multi-stakeholder strategy, not a single-team retro.

**Hybrid notes:** Use one digital board as source of truth; assign online advocate; online-first speaking order in plenaries.

## Scaling guide

### Time budget

| Time budget | Blocks | Detail level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ≤30 min | 2-4 | Agenda table + compact per-block notes |
| 45-90 min | 4-6 | Full blocks with ASCII frames |
| 2-4 hours | 6-10 | Full plan + preparation + hybrid section |
| 1-2 days | 2 day arcs, 8-12 blocks/day | Full plan + materials list + co-facilitation roles |

A 30-minute meeting is not a two-day Strategy Lab. Drop icebreakers, breakouts, and multi-round synthesis when time is short.

### Headcount (independent of time)

| Headcount | Participation structure |
| --- | --- |
| ≤8 | Plenary rounds OK |
| 9-15 | Pairs/triads + popcorn; delegate report-outs |
| 16-30 | Small tables + delegate report-out (no full round-robins) |
| 30+ | World Cafe / Open Space Technology / 25/10 Crowdsourcing / Spokescouncil |

### Timing realism

- Per-block timed-flow steps sum to **≤ ~85%** of block duration; reserve ~2-3 min per block for instructions + transitions.
- Mandatory break every ~60-75 min in-person, ~45-60 min online.
- Every plan carries a cut list: "if running long, cut X / protect Y."
- Times are clock minutes (`h:mm`), not decimals — prevents base-60 overflow (e.g. no `0:55-0:68`).

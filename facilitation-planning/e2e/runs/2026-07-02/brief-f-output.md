## Goal

In one 3-hour in-person session with 25 participants, surface the **top 5 concerns** about the upcoming reorganization and agree on **themes to escalate to leadership** — with enough synthesis that leadership hears a coherent message, not 25 separate complaints.

## Decision authority

- **Not a decision session.** Leadership owns reorg decisions; this forum produces **prioritized input and escalation themes**.
- **Sponsor:** HR / change lead (or executive sponsor) receives the escalation brief; commits to a **leadership response timeline** at close.
- **Acceptance:** Group agrees the top 5 and escalation themes represent their collective voice **for escalation purposes** — not consensus that the reorg is good or bad.

## Key outcomes

- Table-level concerns harvested across the org slice represented in the room
- Clustered themes with delegate synthesis (no 25-person round-robin)
- **Top 5 concerns** dot-voted with transparent criteria
- Escalation brief: themes, supporting evidence quotes (anonymous where collected), and requested leadership responses
- Named scribe deliverable and leadership checkpoint date

## Acceptance criteria

- **Done:** Five concern statements written as "We need leadership to know…"; 3–5 escalation themes with one sentence each on why they matter; sponsor confirms receipt and response date.
- **Fallback if voting splits:** Document top 8 with vote counts; leadership brief highlights top 5 plus "honorable mention" cluster; schedule 45-min leadership Q&A within 2 weeks.

## Confidentiality

- **Chatham House** in the room for personal stories; escalation brief uses **themes and anonymized quotes** unless participant opts in to attribution.
- **Not recorded.** Photos of theme clusters only, not faces.

## Per-block detail

### Block: Arrive and frame (0:00–0:15)

**Why:** 25 people need a shared question and explicit limits — this is input for leadership, not a vote to stop the reorg.

**Format:** [ToP Focused Conversation](https://www.top-network.org/use-focused-conversation) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FORUM QUESTION                                              │
│  "What are our top concerns about the reorg, and what must    │
│   leadership hear?"                                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ESCALATION CHARTER                                          │
│  ✓ Top 5 concerns + themes for leadership                    │
│  ✗ This room does not decide org design                      │
│  ✓ Sponsor commits to response timeline today                │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ROOM: 5 tables × 5 people · table hosts · scribes           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Mic the sponsor for 2 minutes: how input will be used. ORIENT: "What have you heard about the reorg?" (popcorn, max 5 voices — **not** full round-robin). REFLECT: "What is at stake for you?" — table buzz 3 min, one report per table. No org-chart debate in plenary.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Welcome; room map; roles (host, scribe, delegate) |
| 2 | 4 min | Read forum question + charter; sponsor speaks |
| 3 | 5 min | Table buzz REFLECT; one headline per table |
| 4 | 3 min | INTERPRET: Facilitator names 3 stakes themes on main board |

**Block output:** Forum question, charter, table assignments, stakes themes.

### Block: Table check-in (0:15–0:25)

**Why:** Human arrival at tables without a 25-person go-around.

**Format:** [Check-in](https://toolbox.hyperisland.com/check-in-questions) (table-level)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TABLE CHECK-IN (at tables only)                             │
│  "Name · Role · One word for how you arrive"                 │
│  Table scribe notes energy: low / med / high                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Run simultaneously at all tables — 10 min total. Table host keeps pace; no plenary share unless one table requests facilitator support. Assign **table delegate** for later spokescouncil.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Announce prompt; point to table supplies |
| 2 | 8 min | Tables check in; scribes note energy |
| 3 | 1 min | Signal transition to World Cafe |

**Block output:** Table energy notes for facilitator; delegates identified.

---

### Block: Surface concerns (0:25–1:05)

**Why:** World Cafe rotates perspectives so concerns are not dominated by the loudest table or seniority corner.

**Format:** [World Cafe](https://theworldcafe.com/key-concepts-resources/world-cafe-method/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORLD CAFE — 2 rounds × 15 min + harvest                    │
│  Q1: "What worries you most about the reorg?"                │
│  Q2: "What impact are you seeing or anticipating?"           │
│  Table cloth / paper: build on prior table's notes           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Five tables stay put; **participants rotate** 2 people clockwise between rounds (World Cafe host stays). Table scribe captures stickies; host summarizes before rotate. Bell at 15 min. Plenary harvest: each table **one minute** headline — not 25 speeches.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Explain rotation; post questions |
| 2 | 15 min | Round 1 at tables |
| 3 | 3 min | Rotate participants |
| 4 | 15 min | Round 2 at tables |
| 5 | 9 min | Table headline report-outs (5 × ~1.5 min) |

**Block output:** Table papers with concern stickies; five headline themes on main board.

---

### Block: Break (1:05–1:15)

**Why:** Mandatory in-person break (~60–75 min cadence).

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

**Facilitator instruction:** 10 min hard break. Scribes photograph table papers during break.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 min | Break; collect table papers to synthesis station |

**Block output:** Photos of table harvests.

### Block: Synthesize themes (1:15–1:45)

**Why:** 25 people's raw input must become clusterable themes before voting — spokescouncil prevents plenary chaos.

**Format:** [Spokescouncil](https://www.seedsforchange.org.uk/spokescouncil) + [Affinity Clustering](https://www.luma-institute.com/affinity-clustering/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SPOKESCOUNCIL: 5 delegates + facilitator at front           │
│  Delegates cluster table stickies → name themes              │
│  Affinity wall: 10–15 theme cards                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Delegates work at front wall while others rest or silent gallery watch. Delegates may consult tables with **2-min huddles** twice max. Target 10–15 theme cards with plain-language titles ("Role clarity," not "RACI confusion"). No merging themes to dilute minority concerns — keep separate cards if delegates disagree.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Brief delegates; others observe or silent review |
| 2 | 18 min | Delegates affinity cluster all table input |
| 3 | 5 min | Delegate huddle #1 if stuck |
| 4 | 5 min | Name theme cards aloud; confirm with thumbs |
| 5 | 5 min | Gallery walk — add missing stickies to parking lot only |

**Block output:** 10–15 named theme cards on main wall.

---

### Block: Prioritize top 5 (1:45–2:05)

**Why:** Leadership needs a forced rank, not an unprioritized laundry list — criteria before dots.

**Format:** [Dot Vote](https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/methodology/phase4-decide/dot-vote) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PRIORITIZATION CRITERIA (agree first — 3 min)               │
│  "Most harm if ignored" · "Most widespread" · "Most urgent"  │
│  DOTS: 5 per person on theme cards (stack allowed)           │
│  TOP 5 by votes → draft concern statements                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Read criteria aloud; 3 min thumbs. Silent dot vote on theme cards — **individual dots**, not table consensus (prevents one table dominating). Ties at position 5: brief runoff with 2 dots each on tied cards only. Scribe drafts "We need leadership to know…" for top 5.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Agree prioritization criteria on board |
| 2 | 5 min | Silent dot vote (5 dots/person) |
| 3 | 4 min | Count; announce top 5; runoff if needed |
| 4 | 3 min | Scribe reads draft concern statements; thumbs adjust wording |

**Block output:** Top 5 concern statements with vote counts visible.

---

### Block: Escalation themes (2:05–2:35)

**Why:** Leadership needs actionable themes (what to address), not only problems — delegate rounds keep scale manageable.

**Format:** [Rounds](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/rounds.html) (delegate report-outs) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ESCALATION THEMES (3–5)                                     │
│  Theme │ Why leadership must respond │ Requested response     │
│  Delegates propose in round; plenary adds one amendment pass │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Delegates propose 1 escalation theme each in round (5 min). Plenary: popcorn additions merged by facilitator — max 5 themes. Link each theme to at least one of the top 5 concerns. Avoid solution design — frame as "leadership question."

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 min | Delegate round — one escalation theme each |
| 2 | 10 min | Plenary merge to 3–5 themes; scribe captures |
| 3 | 10 min | Pair tables: draft "requested leadership response" line per theme |

**Block output:** 3–5 escalation themes with requested response lines.

---

### Block: Close and handoff (2:35–2:50)

**Why:** Sponsor commitment and a tangible escalation brief — Open Items List adapted for leadership handoff, not team tasks.

**Format:** [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24) (escalation brief)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ESCALATION BRIEF (outline)                                  │
│  • Top 5 concerns (with vote counts)                         │
│  • 3–5 escalation themes + requested responses               │
│  • Parking lot (out of scope today)                          │
│  • Sponsor: response by [DATE] · format [town hall / memo]   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Sponsor reads back what they will deliver to leadership and **when**. Participants thumbs on "good enough to escalate." No new concerns opened — parking lot only.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Scribe reads brief outline from board |
| 2 | 5 min | Sponsor commits to timeline and format |
| 3 | 5 min | Thumbs consent; thank participants |

**Block output:** Escalation brief outline; sponsor commitment on record.

---

### Block: Buffer (2:50–3:00)

**Why:** Absorb overrun; checkout without starting new work.

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

**Facilitator instruction:** Optional one-word checkout at tables (not plenary). Facilitator stays for individual conversations — route HR-sensitive items privately.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 min | Table checkout or open buffer |

**Block output:** Parking lot routed to sponsor.

## Cut list

If running long:

- **Cut:** Shorten World Cafe to one round (drop Q2); reduce escalation theme pair work to facilitator draft from delegates only; skip buffer checkout.
- **Protect:** Frame and escalation charter; World Cafe or equivalent table harvest (minimum one round); criteria-before-dot-vote; top 5 selection; sponsor timeline commitment.

If running short:

- **Add:** Gallery walk comments (5 min) after synthesis; optional anonymous quote capture on index cards during break.

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Room for 5 tables of 5; cafe tablecloths or large paper per table; sticky notes (3 colors); markers; main wall for affinity; dot stickers (5 per person + extras); bell; printed role cards (host, scribe, delegate).

**Pre-read / pre-work:** Send reorg announcement memo and FAQ 72h ahead — label known unknowns so forum time is not spent re-reading announcements. Optional anonymous pre-survey (no login) to seed themes for delegates — not required.

**Facilitator role:** Lead facilitator + co-fac for room logistics; **five table hosts** (briefed 15 min before start); roving scribe for main wall; sponsor present entire session.

**Parking lot:** Separate flip chart; items out of scope for top 5 but captured for leadership appendix.

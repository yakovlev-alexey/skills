# Two-Day Hybrid Strategy Lab — Full Facilitation Plan

## Goal

Agree on exactly **five strategic priorities for the next quarter**, each with a **named owner**, a **measurable success signal**, and a **first action within existing authority** — without implying or committing to budget increases.

## Decision authority

| Field | Assumption (confirm with sponsor before Day 1) |
| --- | --- |
| **Sponsor / decision owner** | Executive sponsor (e.g. CEO, GM, or division head) who can ratify priorities and assign owners; must be **present both days** |
| **Decision rule** | Selected in opening via [Agreement and Certainty](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/agreement-and-certainty): **consent** ([Sociocracy consent decision-making](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/consent-decision-making.html)) for the final five — "good enough for now, safe enough to try," with paramount objections integrated |
| **Will this stick?** | Yes, if sponsor consents publicly and publishes the one-pager within 48h. Soup/escalate items are **explicitly not** part of the five commitments |
| **Facilitator neutrality** | External or internal neutral facilitator; sponsor opens (5 min) but does **not** steer votes or pre-announce priorities |

## Key outcomes

- Shared picture of current reality, strategic purpose, and non-negotiable constraints (including flat budget)
- 8–12 strategic theme clusters from Day 1 exploration, narrowed to **exactly five** priorities with explicit trade-offs and a visible **DROP / not-now** list
- Five priorities stress-tested (premortem), scoped to **control circle** actions only; org-dependent items parked without budget promise
- Consented priority set with five owners, success signals, and [15% Solutions](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/15-percent-solutions) first steps
- Published artifact set and follow-up cadence so commitments survive after the lab

## Acceptance criteria

**Done looks like:**
- Five priority cards on the board, each with: name, owner, success signal, first 15% action, due date
- Consent recorded (or documented dissent that does not block)
- Minimum five items on the DROP / not-now list
- Parking lot / soup items tagged `#escalate` with sponsor receipt — **not** smuggled into Q commitments

**Fallback if no agreement emerges:**
- Document ranked preferences (top 8) + unresolved objections
- Sponsor decides top 5 within 48h using documented criteria and DROP list
- Facilitator memo states: "Input-gathering completed; final five assigned by sponsor" — do not pretend group consent that did not happen

## Confidentiality / recording

- **Default:** Chatham House — themes and decisions may be shared externally; **no attribution** of individual quotes or dissent without permission
- **Recording:** Optional for Day 2 decision blocks only; announce before recording; remote participants must be visible in gallery if recorded
- **Artifacts:** Internal leadership wiki; one-pager may go org-wide; parking lot / soup items **sponsor-only** until resolved

## Per-block detail

### Block: Opening frame (09:00–09:20, Day 1)

**Why:** Align 25 stakeholders on why they are here, what "done" looks like, and what facilitators will not allow (budget promises, back-channel deals).

**Format:** [ToP Focused Conversation](https://www.top-network.org/use-focused-conversation) + [Agreement and Certainty](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/agreement-and-certainty)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DECISION QUESTION                                           │
│  "Which 5 priorities will we commit to this quarter,         │
│   each with an owner — within current budget?"               │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  CONSTRAINT CHARTER (visible all day)                        │
│  ✓ Reprioritize, stop, reshape within existing resources     │
│  ✗ No new budget commitments from this room                  │
│  ✓ Escalation paths for org-dependent items → parking lot    │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ORIENT │ REFLECT │ INTERPRET │ DECIDE (today = Orient only) │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Calm, neutral tone. Name political sensitivity: "Disagreement is expected; we are choosing focus, not winners." Read constraint charter aloud; thumbs consent on board (not show of hands). Run Agreement and Certainty (3 min): "This is high-stakes, moderate certainty — consent fits." Do not let executives pre-announce priorities. **No plenary round-robin at 25 people** — use popcorn/table harvest instead.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Welcome; co-fac roles; hybrid parity rules (board = truth) |
| 2 | 5 min | Read decision question + constraint charter; board thumbs check |
| 3 | 5 min | Agreement and Certainty: confirm consent as Day 2 decision rule |
| 4 | 5 min | ORIENT: pre-read summary + 2–3 popcorn additions (online first) |

**Block output:** Decision question, constraint charter, and decision rule pinned in Miro Frame zone; parking lot column opened.

### Block: Safety baseline (09:20–09:35, Day 1)

**Why:** High political sensitivity requires knowing whether honest dissent is possible before hopes/fears and prioritization.

**Format:** [Safety Check](https://www.funretrospectives.com/safety-check/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Safety (1–5, anonymous on board)   │
│  [|||||||||||||||||||||||||||||]    │
│  Avg: ___   If <3.5 → adjust depth  │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** **Real anonymity** — anonymous Miro widget or private form aggregated by scribe, not show of hands. If average < 3.5, add [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected) (10 min) and shorten World Cafe to two rounds. Never press individuals to explain low scores.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Explain scale; silent anonymous score on board |
| 2 | 2 min | Reveal aggregate only; state adjustment plan if low |
| 3 | 10 min | (Conditional) HSR if score low — otherwise proceed |

**Block output:** Safety score recorded; facilitation depth note on board.

### Block: Arrive and surface stakes (09:35–10:15, Day 1)

**Why:** Help participants arrive as people; surface optimism and anxiety before strategic debate.

**Format:** [Check-in](https://toolbox.hyperisland.com/check-in-questions) + [Hopes and Fears](https://www.teamretro.com/retro-template/hopes-and-fears-retrospective/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
│     HOPES        │     FEARS        │
│  (sticky each)   │  (sticky each)   │
├──────────────────┴──────────────────┤
│  CHECK-IN prompt (table-level):       │
│  "What needs to be true for this      │
│   quarter to feel worth it?"          │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Check-in at **table level** (5 tables × 5 people), not plenary round-robin. One delegate shares table theme (online table first). Silent brainwriting for Hopes/Fears (2 min per column). Cluster quickly; do not debate. Flag budget-related fears to parking lot `#budget`.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 min | Table check-in → 5 delegate report-outs (2 min each, online first) |
| 2 | 5 min | Silent Hopes stickies (all, including remote) |
| 3 | 5 min | Silent Fears stickies |
| 4 | 15 min | Pair share at tables → delegates post themes; facilitator quick affinity |

**Block output:** Hopes/Fears clusters on board; check-in themes in margin.

### Block: Shared context (10:30–11:45, Day 1)

**Why:** Establish a common factual base before purpose and exploration; reduce talking past each other.

**Format:** [What, So What, Now What](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-so-what-now-what)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┐
│    WHAT     │   SO WHAT   │  NOW WHAT   │
│  (facts)    │  (meaning)  │ (questions) │
├─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┤
│             │             │             │
└─────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** WHAT = pre-read metrics + live corrections only (no strategy). SO WHAT = implications, no solutions. NOW WHAT = questions for World Cafe, not decisions. Cut solutioning firmly.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15 min | WHAT: pre-read summary + 5 min corrections/additions (board) |
| 2 | 20 min | SO WHAT: table groups (mixed room/online) → 1 insight each |
| 3 | 15 min | NOW WHAT: collect open questions; **criteria:** relevance to purpose + resource reality → vote top 6 for World Cafe |
| 4 | 15 min | Delegate report-out: confirm shared picture; facilitator reads back |

**Block output:** Three columns filled; top 6 World Cafe questions selected.

### Block: Strategic purpose (12:00–13:00, Day 1)

**Why:** Anchor prioritization in purpose so political factions don't each smuggle in pet priorities.

**Format:** [9 Whys](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/nine-whys)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Start: "Why prioritize now?"       │
│  → Why? → Why? … (up to 9)            │
│  PURPOSE STATEMENT (draft):           │
│  "We exist to __________ so that ___" │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Run in five mixed groups (3 room tables + 2 online breakouts). Each group chains whys from shared starting challenge. Merge via gallery walk + dot vote on phrases, then wordsmith pair — not open debate. Purpose must not assume new budget.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Frame starting challenge from NOW WHAT column |
| 2 | 25 min | Small groups: 9 Whys on board template |
| 3 | 15 min | Gallery walk (digital); dot top purpose phrases |
| 4 | 15 min | Facilitator + volunteer wordsmith; read draft purpose |

**Block output:** One purpose statement on board header (used all Day 2).

### Block: Hold tensions (14:00–15:00, Day 1)

**Why:** Name opposing truths (growth vs flat budget, central vs local, speed vs quality) so they don't explode during voting.

**Format:** [Wicked Questions](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/wicked-questions)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WICKED QUESTION template:                   │
│  "How can we ___ AND ___ at the same time?"  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WQ1: deliver growth without new budget      │
│  WQ2: [group-generated]                      │
│  WQ3: [group-generated]                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Seed WQ1 from constraint charter. Triads (mixed) craft 2 more from Hopes/Fears. No resolving today — hold paradox. Redirect "we need more money" to "what would we stop to fund this internally?"

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 min | Explain wicked questions; seed budget WQ |
| 2 | 25 min | Triads: draft 2 WQs each → board |
| 3 | 15 min | Dot vote top 3 WQs to keep visible Day 2 |
| 4 | 10 min | Read WQs aloud; acknowledge tension without fixing |

**Block output:** Three wicked questions pinned; linked to World Cafe rounds.

| 3 | 20 min | Round 2 (rotate) |
| 4 | 20 min | Round 3 (rotate) |
| 5 | 15 min | Host summaries → sticky harvest to backlog wall |

### Block: Narrow to five (11:45–12:30)

**Why:** Transparent, defensible narrowing to exactly five priorities under constraint.

**Format:** [Dot Vote](https://designsprintkit.withgoogle.com/methodology/phase4-decide/dot-vote) + [ToP Collaborative Priority Setting](https://www.top-network.org/set-priorities-collaboratively)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CRITERIA (set BEFORE vote):        │
│  1) Advances purpose                │
│  2) Achievable without new budget   │
│  3) Has credible owner candidate    │
│  4) Coherent as a set of 5          │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Themes → Dot vote (5 dots/person)  │
│  → Top 8 → Must/Should/Could/Drop   │
│  → Final Top 5                      │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** **Criteria on board before dots.** All voting via Miro — no show of hands. After dot vote, sort top 8 into Must/Should/Could/Drop. Facilitator proposes five from Must; group refines — no new items. DROP list is as important as top 5.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Read and confirm criteria; thumbs on board |
| 2 | 10 min | Dot vote on theme clusters |
| 3 | 15 min | Top 8 → Must/Should/Could/Drop sort |
| 4 | 15 min | Negotiation to exactly 5 Must-haves |

**Block output:** Top 5 priority cards + visible deprioritized list (min 5 items).

### Block: Scope within control (14:30–15:15)

**Why:** Separate what the group can commit to without budget from what requires escalation — directly addresses political sensitivity.

**Format:** [Circles & Soup / Circle of Influence](https://retromat.org/en/?id=29)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
        ┌─────────────┐
        │  CONTROL    │  ← 15% actions, owners here
        │  (circle)   │
        ├─────────────┤
        │  INFLUENCE  │  ← needs alignment, no budget promise
        ├─────────────┤
        │  CONCERN    │  ← soup: parking lot / escalate
        │  (soup)     │
        └─────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Each priority: classify proposed actions. Soup items tagged `#escalate` go to sponsor after session — not into Q commitments. Say aloud: "Soup is not failure; it's honest scoping."

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Explain circles; demo one priority |
| 2 | 30 min | Table work: classify actions for all 5 |
| 3 | 10 min | Delegate report: control vs soup counts |

**Block output:** Five priorities with control-ring actions marked "committed"; soup in parking lot.

### Block: Lock commitments (15:30–16:00)

**Why:** Move from consensus-by-silence to explicit consent with room for objections.

**Format:** [Sociocracy consent decision-making](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/consent-decision-making.html) + [Resolve Objections](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/resolve-objections.html)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PROPOSAL: "We adopt these 5       │
│  priorities for next quarter with    │
│  named owners and no new budget      │
│  claims from this room."             │
│  Objections → integrate or amend     │
│  Consent: no paramount objections    │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Read proposal once. Clarifying questions → reactions → objections (paramount only). Use Resolve Objections (max 5 min each). **Executives speak last** in objection round. Online speakers first.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Read proposal + top 5 list |
| 2 | 10 min | Clarifying questions (popcorn, online first) |
| 3 | 10 min | Objections round; integrate amendments |
| 4 | 5 min | Confirm consent; record non-blocking dissent |

**Block output:** Consented priority list; amendment log; dissent notes (Chatham House).

- Day 1: Wicked Questions → seed only WQ1 + one group-generated WQ (save 20 min)
- Day 1: World Cafe → two rounds instead of three (save 20 min)
- Day 2: Cover Story Mock-up → headline only, no quote/subhead (save 15 min)
- Day 2: Premortem → top 3 priorities only, not all five (save 15 min)
- Any block: table report-outs → 1 min instead of 2–3 min

**Protect (non-negotiable):**
- Opening frame + constraint charter + decision rule
- Safety Check (and HSR downshift if score low)
- Criteria-before-vote in Narrow to five

## Preparation

**Space / tools:**
- Room: cabaret seating (5 tables of 5), projector mirroring Miro, wall space for constraint charter printout
- Hybrid AV: ceiling mic, 2 room cameras, always-on gallery view, chat monitored
- Digital board: Miro — zones: Frame, Hopes/Fears, WSW, Purpose, WQ, Cafe tables, Themes, Impact maps, Covers, Votes, Top 5, Risks, Circles, Actions, Parking lot
- Materials: visible timer, printed constraint charter at each table, name tents with role/team
- Pre-build board templates 48h ahead; test remote dot-voting and breakouts

**Pre-read / pre-work (send 5 business days before):**
- 2-page context brief: last quarter results, **explicit flat budget reality**, known exec constraints
- Each participant submits one "strategic bet" via form (seeds World Cafe)
- Optional: Agreement and Certainty self-reflection — "What kind of decision is this for you?"
- Sponsor pre-brief: no budget promises from the room; escalation path for soup items documented

**Facilitator roles:**
- **Lead facilitator:** arc, time, consent process, executive dynamics
- **Co-facilitator (room):** table coaching, parking lot, energy
- **Online advocate (mandatory):** remote parity, chat/hands, reads room comments aloud
- **Scribe:** live board hygiene, action table, end-of-day export
- **Executive sponsor:** opens Day 1 (5 min), receives soup list Day 2; does not steer votes

**Parking lot:**
- Miro column `#parking-lot`; triggers: budget asks, HR/legal, interpersonal, out-of-quarter scope
- Revisit: end of each day (5 min), Circles & Soup block, final Open Items List
- Sponsor receives `#escalate` items within 24h — not bundled into the five priorities

## Hybrid / online parity

**Digital board:** Miro — single source of truth. Room uses projected board; flipchart work photographed into Miro within 5 minutes.

**Online group:** Seven participants distributed across **mixed breakouts every session** — never isolated as "the online table" except when hosting a World Cafe round. Breakouts via Zoom/Teams with Miro deep links.

**Parity rules:**
- If it is not on the board, it did not happen
- Online speakers first in all plenaries, objection rounds, and table report-outs
- All voting via board dots — no show of hands in room
- Co-fac repeats room comments near mic for online
- Gallery walks: 30 sec silent board read for remote before discussion
- No side conversations deciding priorities; chat for clarifications only during votes

**Co-facilitation:**
- Online advocate monitors chat, raised hands, connection issues; authority to pause for parity
- Scribe reads new in-room stickies aloud
- Lead + online advocate debrief 15 min after Day 1; adjust Day 2 table assignments

# Team Retrospective After a Rough Sprint — Facilitation Plan

**Arc selected:** `retro-safe` (low trust, rough sprint, psychological safety is the primary risk; action commitment comes only after safety is established)

**Session:** 75 minutes · 7 participants · online only · neutral facilitator recommended

---

## Goal

Help the team reflect honestly on a difficult sprint in a psychologically safe way and leave with **2–3 concrete, team-owned improvement actions** they can actually try next sprint.

## Decision authority

| Element | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Sponsor / owner** | Team collectively owns retro outcomes; **Scrum Master or team lead** sponsors follow-through and removes blockers |
| **Decision rule** | **Consent** on improvement experiments — “good enough for now, safe enough to try” for team-owned actions ([Sociocracy consent decision-making](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/consent-decision-making.html) as reference if objections arise) |
| **Authority reality** | Actions within the team’s control will stick if owners commit; anything requiring manager, other teams, or org policy is **input for escalation**, not a retro commitment |
| **Facilitator neutrality** | Facilitator should **not** be a party to the main interpersonal tensions from the sprint. If the requester is involved in conflict, bring a neutral co-facilitator or external facilitator |

## Key outcomes

- A **named safety baseline** — team knows whether honest discussion is possible today
- **Shared themes** from the sprint (feelings + patterns), captured on the board without blame
- **2–3 improvement actions** with owner, due date, and success signal
- **Parking lot** for interpersonal or org issues that need a separate conversation

## Acceptance criteria

| Done looks like | Fallback if not met |
| --- | --- |
| Safety Check completed; average ≥ 3/5 or team agrees to proceed with anonymous modes | **Safety downshift:** switch to [Creating Safety](https://www.funretrospectives.com/creating-safety/) or [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected); **drop action-forcing**; end with listening summary + schedule repair session |
| At least 2 actions in [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24) format with owners | If no agreement: capture **experiments to discuss async**; facilitator schedules 30-min follow-up within 3 business days |
| Every participant spoke at least once in the first 15 minutes | Use chat + sticky notes; do not rely on verbal round-robin under pressure |

## Per-block detail

### Block: Frame & working agreements (0:00–0:05)

**Why:** Set psychological safety expectations before anyone evaluates the sprint. Low-trust teams need explicit norms and permission to pass.

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  RETRO: Sprint [N] — rough sprint, learning not blame       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Working agreements:                                        │
│  • Focus on systems & behaviors, not people                 │
│  • One mic — use reactions / raise hand                     │
│  • Pass is always OK                                        │
│  • What's said here: [Chatham House — see artifacts]        │
│  • Parking lot → column on board                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

- Tone: calm, non-defensive, curious.
- Say: *“We had a rough sprint. Today we’re here to understand what happened and what we can change — not to assign blame. If at any point this doesn’t feel safe, say so; we can change format.”*
- Avoid: defending leadership, minimizing (“it wasn’t that bad”), forcing verbal sharing.
- Confirm: **not recorded** unless team explicitly opts in (default: no recording).

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Welcome; state goal and 75-min arc |
| 2 | 2 min | Read working agreements; ask thumbs-up / adjust in chat |
| 3 | 1 min | Point to board zones; assign scribe if not pre-assigned |

**Block output:** Agreements visible on board header; parking lot column created.

### Block: Safety & arrival (0:05–0:12)

**Why:** Test whether honest discussion is possible **before** inspecting the sprint. [One Word](https://www.funretrospectives.com/one-word/) gets everyone speaking early in a low-risk way.

**Format:** [Safety Check](https://www.funretrospectives.com/safety-check/) + [One Word](https://www.funretrospectives.com/one-word/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
│  Safety (1–5)        │  One Word — sprint   │
│  [anonymous poll]    │  (sticky each)       │
├──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤
│  Avg: ___            │  tired / tense / …   │
│  Proceed? Y / Downshift│                     │
└──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

- Run Safety Check as **anonymous poll** in the video tool or board (real anonymity — not “everyone watches you type”).
- Scale: 1 = “I’ll stay silent” → 5 = “I can speak openly.”
- If **average < 3** or **≥2 people at 1–2**: trigger **safety downshift** (see Cut list / contingency). Do **not** proceed to Starfish or action items.
- One Word: each person adds one sticky **without explanation**; no debate.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain Safety Check; launch anonymous poll |
| 2 | 2 min | Reveal aggregate only; ask “Proceed or adjust?” in chat |
| 3 | 2 min | One Word — silent sticky, then quick popcorn read (optional) |

**Block output:** Safety score recorded; One Word stickies on board; proceed/downshift decision noted.

**Safety downshift (if triggered):**

- Replace remaining agenda with [Creating Safety](https://www.funretrospectives.com/creating-safety/) (15 min) or [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected) (20 min).
- End with: what would need to be true for a fuller retro; schedule follow-up; **no forced action list**.

### Block: Release & reflect (0:12–0:27)

**Why:** After a rough sprint with low trust, people often carry unspoken anxiety. [Stinky Fish](https://hyperisland.com/en/blog/thought-leadership/stinky-fish) surfaces “what I’m carrying”; [Draw Your Feelings](https://www.funretrospectives.com/draw-your-feelings/) connects emotion to the sprint without requiring articulate verbal confession.

**Format:** [Stinky Fish](https://hyperisland.com/en/blog/thought-leadership/stinky-fish) + [Draw Your Feelings](https://www.funretrospectives.com/draw-your-feelings/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌─────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
│  Stinky Fish            │  Draw your feelings       │
│  "Anxiety I'm carrying" │  (sketch / metaphor)      │
│  [private → optional    │  Link to sprint moment    │
│   share to board]       │  [private or team zone]   │
├─────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┤
│  Optional: cluster similar feelings (no names)      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

- **Anonymity honesty:** Stinky Fish starts **private** (individual sticky only visible to self, or anonymous board mode). Sharing to team zone is **opt-in**, not mandatory.
- Draw Your Feelings: stick figures / weather / battery — **no art skill required**.
- Do not ask “who wrote this?” or press for elaboration.
- If energy is very low: skip verbal debrief; only cluster stickies silently.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Intro Stinky Fish prompt; silent private write |
| 2 | 3 min | Opt-in: drag to shared zone OR keep private |
| 3 | 2 min | Intro Draw Your Feelings; silent draw |
| 4 | 4 min | Opt-in share; facilitator clusters themes |
| 5 | 2 min | Mirror 2–3 themes: “I’m hearing …” — no fixing |

**Block output:** Themed clusters on board; private stickies may remain private.

---

### Block: Break (0:27–0:32)

**Why:** Mandatory online break (~45–60 min cadence). Emotional work is taxing; break reduces reactivity before problem-solving.

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

**Facilitator instruction:** Start timer; cameras optional off; keep board open for late stickies. Return prompt in chat at 4:30.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Break — no agenda |

**Block output:** Team reset; board unchanged.

---

### Block: Inspect patterns (0:32–0:47)

**Why:** Move from feelings to **system patterns** using [Starfish](https://www.teamretro.com/retro-template/starfish-retrospective) — richer than Start/Stop/Continue, still action-oriented later. Silent brainwriting first protects quieter voices in a low-trust room.

**Format:** [Starfish](https://www.teamretro.com/retro-template/starfish-retrospective)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
│ Keep     │ Less     │ More     │ Start    │ Stop     │
│ doing    │ of       │ of       │ doing    │ doing    │
├──────────┼──────────┼──────────┼──────────┼──────────┤
│ sticky   │ sticky   │ sticky   │ sticky   │ sticky   │
│ …        │ …        │ …        │ …        │ …        │
└──────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┘
        → Affinity cluster duplicates across columns
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

- Frame: *“Systems and habits — what helped, what hurt, what should change?”*
- **Silent brainwriting first** (no talking) — reduces groupthink and manager voice dominance.
- Cluster duplicates; **do not** debate each sticky.
- Interpersonal attacks → parking lot: *“Valid topic, wrong format — we’ll note it for a separate conversation.”*

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Explain Starfish columns |
| 2 | 6 min | Silent individual stickies (aim 2–4 each) |
| 3 | 4 min | Read stickies aloud briefly OR delegate read from chat |
| 4 | 3 min | Cluster similar items; name 3–5 theme headers |

**Block output:** Clustered Starfish board with theme labels.

---

### Block: Prioritize (0:47–0:58)

**Why:** Narrow many themes to what the team will actually work on. [Dot Voting - Starfish](https://retromat.org/en/?id=49) makes priority visible and fair.

**Format:** [Dot Voting - Starfish](https://retromat.org/en/?id=49)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  THEME CLUSTERS (from Starfish)                             │
│  [A] Communication breakdown    [B] Scope churn             │
│  [C] Testing / quality          [D] Support / handoffs      │
│  ...                                                        │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Vote: 3 dots per person · cannot stack all on one theme    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

**Decision criteria (state BEFORE voting):**

1. **Impact** — would this materially improve next sprint?
2. **Team control** — can *we* change this without waiting on others?
3. **Safety** — is the team willing to try this experiment?

- 3 dots per person; max 2 dots on any single theme.
- Voting in **board**, not show of hands (online parity).
- Top **3–5** themes advance; rest → parking lot / future retro.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | State criteria; demo voting in board |
| 2 | 3 min | Silent dot vote |
| 3 | 3 min | Count; announce top themes |
| 4 | 2 min | Quick sense-check: “Surprises? Missing critical theme?” |

**Block output:** Ranked top 3–5 themes marked on board.

---

### Block: Commit & close (0:58–1:15)

**Why:** Convert themes into **owned experiments**. [Circles & Soup](https://retromat.org/en/?id=29) filters out org-level blockers the team cannot own; [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24) forces owner + date; [Maximize Follow Through](https://retromat.org/en/?id=117) improves odds past actions failed.

**Format:** [Circles & Soup / Circle of Influence](https://retromat.org/en/?id=29) + [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24) + [Maximize Follow Through](https://retromat.org/en/?id=117)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Circle of Influence                                        │
│  ┌─────────────────┐    ┌──────────────────────────────┐   │
│  │ Team can do     │    │ Soup — needs others / org    │   │
│  │ (actions here)  │    │ (escalate, not retro commit) │   │
│  └─────────────────┘    └──────────────────────────────┘   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  OPEN ITEMS LIST                                            │
│  Action | Owner | Due | Success signal | First step       │
│  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  │
│  1.       |       |     |                |                  │
│  2.       |       |     |                |                  │
│  3.       |       |     |                |                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:**

- For each top theme: brainstorm **one small experiment** (1–2 sprint scope), not a permanent policy change.
- Move “needs manager / other team” items to **Soup**; assign **escalation owner** (often SM/lead), not a team action.
- Each action needs: **owner (one name)**, **due date**, **success signal**, **first step this week**.
- [Maximize Follow Through](https://retromat.org/en/?id=117): ask *“What usually stops us following through?”* — add one enabling step per action (calendar hold, slack reminder, etc.).
- Close: [Round of Admiration](https://retromat.org/en/?id=76) optional if 2 min remain — one appreciation each, no debate.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain Circle vs Soup |
| 2 | 5 min | Draft 3–4 candidate actions in inner circle |
| 3 | 3 min | Select **2–3**; assign owners and dates |
| 4 | 3 min | Maximize Follow Through — one enabler per action |
| 5 | 2 min | Read back action table; confirm consent |
| 6 | 2 min | Close: thanks; when summary lands; next checkpoint |

**Block output:** Open Items List with 2–3 actions; Soup list with escalation owners; parking lot preserved.

## Cut list

If running long:

| Cut | Protect |
| --- | --- |
| Shorten Draw Your Feelings to private-only (no share) — save **4 min** | **Safety Check** and proceed/downshift decision |
| Reduce Starfish to **Keep / Stop / Start** only (3 columns) — save **3 min** | **Silent brainwriting** before any debate |
| Skip Round of Admiration at close — save **2 min** | **Open Items List** with owners and dates |
| Limit to **2 actions** instead of 3 — save **3 min** | **Circles & Soup** — do not commit Soup items as team actions |
| If safety is low at 0:12: **drop Starfish + voting entirely** | **Creating Safety** or **Heard, Seen, Respected** + schedule follow-up retro |

If running **short** (team very engaged on one theme): extend Commit block by cutting optional debrief in Reflect block.

## Preparation

**Space / tools:**

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Video | Zoom / Meet / Teams with breakout **disabled** unless needed |
| Board | Miro / FigJam / TeamRetro / EasyRetro — **single source of truth** |
| Timer | Visible shared timer (board widget or screen share) |
| Poll | Anonymous safety poll (tool-native or Miro private mode) |
| Roles | Facilitator (neutral), Scribe (updates board live), **Online advocate** (monitors chat, hands, reads aloud) |

**Pre-read / pre-work (send 24h before):**

```
Subject: Retro tomorrow — 75 min, psychological safety first

Hi team,

Tomorrow we’ll retro Sprint [N]. Goal: learn from a rough sprint and agree 2–3 
small improvements we control.

Please come with:
• One thing that went harder than expected (for your eyes only — no pre-share required)
• Your availability to own a small experiment if we agree one

Defaults unless you object in reply:
• Chatham House — share learnings outside the room, not who said what
• Not recorded
• Pass is always OK

Board link: [URL]
```

**Facilitator role:**

- **Lead facilitator:** time, safety, downshift decisions, does not defend sprint outcomes.
- **Scribe:** captures stickies, action table, parking lot.
- **Online advocate:** *“If it’s not on the board, it didn’t happen”* — reads chat contributions into plenary.

**Parking lot:**

- Dedicated board column: *“Later / other forum.”*
- Interpersonal or HR-sensitive items → note + **separate 1:1 or mediated session**, not forced group discussion today.

## Hybrid / online parity

**Digital board:** Miro, FigJam, TeamRetro, or EasyRetro — one link, edit access for all 7 participants.

**Online group (all remote):**

- No room-only side conversations; use **chat for parallel input**, scribe pulls into board.
- **Anonymous mode** for Safety Check and optional Stinky Fish — verify tool supports real anonymity.
- Dot voting **in board only** — not verbal “hands up.”
- Speaking order: **rotate starting person** each block; online advocate invites quiet names from participant list.

**Parity rules:**

- If it’s not on the board, it didn’t happen.
- Camera optional; **participation via stickies/chat counts**.
- One speaker at a time; use **raise hand / reaction** queue.
- Facilitator reads new chat questions every 3–5 minutes during silent work.
- No “let’s take this offline” without parking-lot capture visible to all.

**Co-facilitation:**

- **Online advocate** monitors: chat, raised hands, stuck participants, tech issues.
- If someone dominates: *“Thank you — let’s get the next voices on the board silently.”*

## Post-session process

| Step | Owner | When | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publish board link + summary + action table | Scribe | Same day (within 4h) | Tag action owners in channel |
| Create tasks / tickets for each action | Action owners | Within 24h | Link tasks to retro doc |
| Escalate Soup items | Scrum Master / team lead | Within 48h | Confirm owner outside team or date for decision |
| Review action progress | Facilitator + team | Daily standup + **5 business days** | Resize, pair, or drop stuck actions |
| Revisit experiments | Whole team | **Next retro** (start of meeting) | Mark done / continue / stop; max 5 min |
| Safety follow-up (if downshifted) | Facilitator + lead | Within 1 week | Schedule repair or mediated session |

**Success signal for this retro:** At next retro, team reports ≥1 action ** tried** and at least one visible process change; Safety Check average **same or higher** than this session.

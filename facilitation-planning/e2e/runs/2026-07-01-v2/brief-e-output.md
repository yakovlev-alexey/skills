## Goal

Help a ten-person cross-functional team move from blame and low trust toward **shared understanding and relational repair** after a missed launch — without forcing a sprint retro or action-item backlog.

## Decision authority

- **Not a decision session.** No platform, scope, or launch decisions today.
- **Facilitator / sponsor:** People lead or engineering manager sponsors the session; **neutral external or HR-adjacent facilitator strongly preferred** if the requester is a party to the conflict.
- **Authority reality:** Outputs are **working agreements and mutual requests**, not commitments to management. Escalation to HR or leadership happens only via explicit participant choice after the session.

## Key outcomes

- Safety baseline measured; facilitation depth adjusted if the room is not ready for dialogue
- Concerns and unspeakable tensions surfaced with **honest anonymity mechanism** where promised
- Both subgroups hear each other's account of the missed launch without debate or fixing
- Cross-role **negotiable requests** (What I Need From You) — not a task list
- Light **relationship agreement** (how we work together next week), or a scheduled follow-up conversation if safety stayed low

## Acceptance criteria

- **Done:** Safety score recorded; concern themes clustered; each participant can name one thing they understand differently about the other subgroup; documented relationship agreement OR explicit "we need another conversation on [date]" with neutral facilitator.
- **Fallback if safety stays low (avg < 3):** **Safety downshift** — drop request/ agreement blocks; extend [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected) and [Conversation Cafe](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/conversation-cafe); close with listening-only summary; **no action items**.

## Confidentiality

- **Chatham House** for session content: themes may leave the room; **no attributing quotes to individuals** in the written summary.
- **Not recorded.** Summary shares themes only, not "Person X said…"

## Per-block detail

### Block: Frame and safety (0:00–0:10)

**Why:** Open conflict after a missed launch requires explicit safety work before any inspection of "what went wrong."

**Format:** [Creating Safety](https://www.funretrospectives.com/creating-safety/) + [Safety Check](https://www.funretrospectives.com/safety-check/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PURPOSE: Understanding + repair — NOT retro actions         │
│  ✗ No action items  ✗ No blame  ✓ Listening  ✓ Requests      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SAFETY CHECK (1–5) — blind poll, not named dots on board    │
│  [Slido/Mentimeter anonymous poll → show aggregate only]     │
│  If avg < 3 → SAFETY DOWNSHIFT (see acceptance criteria)     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** State clearly: "This is not a sprint retro. We will not leave with Jira tickets." Establish Chatham House. **Anonymity honesty:** Safety Check uses a **blind anonymous poll** (Slido, Mentimeter, or Zoom poll with no names) — not colored dots on a Miro frame where cursor labels reveal authors. If average < 3, announce downshift plan immediately and skip blocks 5–6 in favor of extended HSR.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Welcome; purpose vs retro; ground rules on board |
| 2 | 2 min | Creating Safety prompt: "What helps you participate honestly today?" — chat storm, facilitator reads themes |
| 3 | 3 min | Launch blind Safety Check poll; reveal aggregate only |
| 4 | 2 min | State downshift branch if needed; confirm no recording |

**Block output:** Ground rules on board; safety score (number only); downshift flag yes/no.

### Block: Surface concerns (0:10–0:25)

**Why:** Blame cycles hide unspeakable concerns; anonymous writing lowers cost before dialogue.

**Format:** [Writing the Unspeakable](https://retromat.org/en/?id=75) + [Spot the Elephant](https://retromat.org/en/?id=130)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ANONYMOUS INPUT (real mechanism)                            │
│  Google Form / Slido open text — no login, no names         │
│  Facilitator copies themes to board WITHOUT attribution      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SPOT THE ELEPHANT — "What are we not saying about the       │
│  missed launch?" → cluster themes on Miro                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** **Anonymity honesty:** Writing the Unspeakable uses a **no-login Google Form or Slido** — not anonymous Miro stickies (participants' cursors and edit history are visible to board owners). Facilitator paraphrases submissions onto the board; do not read verbatim if it identifies a subgroup attack — soften to theme language. Spot the Elephant: silent Miro dot-vote on which elephant themes resonate (dots can be anonymous if Miro voting mode hides authors — otherwise use poll).

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Share form link; explain true anonymity vs board stickies |
| 2 | 5 min | Silent write in form |
| 3 | 5 min | Facilitator clusters themes on board (paraphrased) |
| 4 | 3 min | Spot the Elephant — vote on top 3 themes via anonymous poll |

**Block output:** Clustered concern themes (no names); top 3 elephants starred.

---

### Block: Shared understanding (0:25–0:45)

**Why:** Conversation Cafe gives equal, slow turns across subgroups without debate — repair requires being heard before requests.

**Format:** [Conversation Cafe](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/conversation-cafe)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CAFE ROUND 1 (subgroup A lens)                              │
│  "What did you experience during the missed launch?"         │
│  CAFE ROUND 2 (subgroup B lens)                              │
│  "What do you think the other group doesn't see?"          │
│  Rules: talk object (virtual) · no cross-talk · no fix       │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LISTENING NOTES (facilitator): theme │ heard from │ not debate│
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Assign random speak order (not subgroup blocks). Online advocate monitors chat for "I need the floor" — unmute in order. If someone attacks, pause: "Return to your experience, not their character." **If safety downshift active:** one cafe round only + 10 min HSR pairs in breakouts.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain cafe rules; name virtual talking piece |
| 2 | 9 min | Round 1 — ~1 min each |
| 3 | 9 min | Round 2 — ~1 min each |
| 4 | 5 min | Facilitator reads back 4–5 themes: "What we heard" |

**Block output:** "What we heard" theme list on board; no counter-arguments captured.

**Why:** Mandatory online cadence break (~45–60 min).

**Format:** plain facilitation structure

**Facilitator instruction:** Encourage cameras off, stretch. No breakout blame conversations — reopen in 5 minutes.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min | Break; scribe snapshots board |

**Block output:** Board snapshot saved.

### Block: Cross-role requests (0:50–1:05)

**Why:** Move from understanding to negotiable asks — distinct from retro action items (behavioral, relational, not "fix the bug").

**Format:** [What I Need From You](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-i-need-from-you)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WHAT I NEED FROM YOU (max 1 request per person)             │
│  From ___ I need ___ so that ___                             │
│  Response: Yes / No / Let's negotiate / Not today            │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ✗ NOT: "Deploy Friday"  ✓ "Heads-up before scope cuts"      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** **Skip entirely if safety downshift active.** Model a relational request. Responses are honest — "not today" is valid. Do not convert requests into Jira tasks. Scribe captures request + response only with permission.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain format; contrast with action items |
| 2 | 10 min | Round — each person one request to another role/subgroup |
| 3 | 3 min | Facilitator confirms: "These are agreements in principle, not sprint commitments" |

**Block output:** Request/response pairs on board (Chatham House summary later).

---

### Block: Relationship agreement (1:05–1:20)

**Why:** Repair needs lightweight norms for the next two weeks — not a backlog.

**Format:** [Rounds](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/rounds.html) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WORKING AGREEMENT (next 2 weeks) — max 5 bullets            │
│  • How we flag risk early                                    │
│  • How we talk when disagreeing                              │
│  • What we won't do (e.g. public blame in Slack)             │
│  Consent: thumb sideways ok                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Draft 3 bullets from cafe themes; round to add one each (max 2 passes). Thumbs for consent — not unanimous enthusiasm required. **If safety downshift:** replace with scheduling a follow-up listening session date.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 min | Propose 3 draft norms from themes |
| 2 | 8 min | Rounds to amend/add |
| 3 | 4 min | Thumbs consent; note who held sideways (no forced explain) |

**Block output:** 3–5 working agreement bullets or follow-up date.

---

### Block: Close (1:20–1:30)

**Why:** End with dignity and acknowledgment — HSR rebuilds minimal relational safety.

**Format:** [Heard, Seen, Respected](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/heard-seen-respected)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HSR (pairs in breakouts, 2 min each direction)              │
│  "One thing I heard from you today that landed"              │
│  CLOSE: No action log · Summary themes only · Parking lot    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Breakout pairs (mixed subgroups if safety ≥ 3). No public sharing unless volunteered. Remind: summary goes to team channel as themes only, same day.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Assign breakouts (5 pairs) |
| 2 | 5 min | HSR in pairs |
| 3 | 3 min | Return; thank group; state summary timeline |
| 4 | 1 min | Parking lot items → sponsor 1:1, not public list |

**Block output:** Verbal close; no action log published.

## Cut list

If running long:

- **Cut:** Shorten cafe to one round; reduce relationship agreement to 3 facilitator-drafted bullets with thumbs only; shorten HSR close to one sentence in chat.
- **Protect:** Safety framing and blind Safety Check; anonymous Writing the Unspeakable; Conversation Cafe (or HSR if downshift); explicit "not a retro" close.

If **safety downshift** triggers:

- **Cut:** Blocks 5–6 entirely; extend Block 3 with HSR breakouts (+15 min).
- **Protect:** Creating Safety, anonymous concern surfacing, listening summary.

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Zoom/Meet with breakout rooms; Miro or FigJam (facilitator-owned); Slido or Mentimeter for blind polls; no-login Google Form for anonymous text; co-facilitator as online advocate.

**Pre-read / pre-work:** None required — pre-read may increase anxiety. Sponsor sends 48h note: purpose, no recording, no performance review linkage.

**Facilitator role:** Neutral facilitator (not the eng manager who owned the launch if they are blamed). Online advocate watches chat, enforces speak order, reads chat questions aloud.

**Parking lot:** Miro parking lot column; interpersonal HR issues → sponsor private channel, not read in plenary.

---

## Hybrid / online parity

**Digital board:** Miro/FigJam is the single source of truth — facilitator copies anonymous form themes onto board; participants do not paste identifiable rants in main chat.

**Online group:** All ten participants remote (online-only session). Breakouts for HSR; cafe in main room with raised-hand queue.

**Parity rules:**

- If it's not on the board, it didn't happen for the group record.
- Anonymous means **blind poll or no-login form** — never "anonymous stickies" on a shared board with visible cursors.
- Chat-first option for introverts: "Type one word for check-in" before optional voice.
- Online advocate calls on raised hands before open discussion; no side conversations in chat during cafe rounds.

**Co-facilitation:** Online advocate monitors chat, breakouts, and tech; lead facilitator runs arc and safety downshift branches.

## Goal

Produce a documented, criteria-based group recommendation on Platform A vs Platform B for Q3 investment — with ranked preferences, explicit dissent, and rationale the CFO can use even if they override the group's preference.

## Decision authority

- **Sponsor / decision owner:** CFO (present); group is the leadership forum whose input informs the final call.
- **Authority reality:** The CFO has signaled they may override whatever the group decides. This session is **structured input-gathering and alignment**, not a binding vote. The group will leave with **documented criteria, scored options, and a ranked preference with reservations** — not the illusion of a locked decision.
- **Decision rule (for this room):** Consult → produce a **recommendation with documented dissent** using [Gradients of Agreement](https://workshopweaver.com/facilitation-methods/gradients-of-agreement). The CFO confirms how input will be used at close (same day or within 48h).
- **Confidentiality:** Chatham House for discussion; **attributed** preference scores and dissent notes in the written artifact (directors are accountable stakeholders, not an anonymous workforce survey).

## Key outcomes

- Shared evaluation criteria for Platform A vs B (weighted, visible on the board)
- Documented facts and trade-offs both platforms (not advocacy pitches)
- Individual scored ranking of A vs B against criteria, aggregated transparently
- Gradients-of-agreement snapshot on the group's recommendation, with named reservations where people cannot support the majority preference
- One-page recommendation memo draft (criteria + ranking + dissent + open questions) ready for CFO review

## Acceptance criteria

- **Done:** Criteria list agreed (≥5 dimensions, weights visible); both platforms scored against criteria; ranked group preference with gradient levels and at least one captured dissent line per reservation level 1–3.
- **Fallback if no preference emerges:** Document split criteria (where A wins vs where B wins), list unresolved questions, and schedule a 30-minute CFO-led decision brief within one week — do not force a false consensus.

## Per-block detail

### Block: Frame and authority (0:00–0:08)

**Why:** Eight directors need explicit agreement on what this meeting can and cannot decide before debating platforms — especially with CFO override risk.

**Format:** [Agreement and Certainty](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/agreement-and-certainty) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DECISION QUESTION                                           │
│  "Which platform should we recommend for Q3 investment —       │
│   and on what criteria?"                                     │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  AUTHORITY REALITY                                           │
│  • Group → documented criteria + ranked recommendation       │
│  • CFO → final decision (may override)                       │
│  • Today ≠ binding vote                                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  AGREEMENT / CERTAINTY MAP (quick placement)               │
│  Where is Platform choice on the matrix? → consult arc       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Neutral tone; name the elephant: "We are not pretending the CFO must adopt our vote." Ask CFO (or their delegate) to confirm in one sentence how today's output will be used. Use thumbs consent on the decision rule. Redirect any "let's just pick A now" to the criteria block.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Welcome; state 90-minute arc and desired artifacts |
| 2 | 3 min | Place issue on Agreement/Certainty matrix; confirm consult → recommend arc |
| 3 | 2 min | Read authority reality aloud; CFO confirms handoff process |
| 4 | 1 min | Write decision question on board; open parking lot column |

**Block output:** Decision question, authority charter, and decision rule visible on wall; parking lot opened.

### Block: Check-in (0:08–0:15)

**Why:** Directors arrive with different stakes (owning teams tied to A or B); naming mood reduces performative certainty.

**Format:** [One Word](https://www.funretrospectives.com/one-word/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ONE WORD — how you arrive today    │
│  [word] [word] [word] ... (8 max)   │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Round-robin, 10 seconds each, no commentary. Facilitator captures words on a strip; do not interpret aloud. If someone says "skeptical" or "committed," note internally for later facilitation — do not probe.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Explain prompt; model with your own word |
| 2 | 5 min | Round-robin around table (8 people × ~30 sec) |
| 3 | 1 min | Read words back once; transition to context block |

**Block output:** Mood strip on board; facilitator private note on energy.

---

### Block: Shared context on platforms (0:15–0:32)

**Why:** Criteria-based ranking requires a shared factual baseline — separate evidence from advocacy before criteria work.

**Format:** [What, So What, Now What](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-so-what-now-what)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
│    WHAT      │   SO WHAT    │  NOW WHAT    │
│  (facts A/B) │  (meaning)   │  (questions) │
├──────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┤
│ Platform A   │              │              │
│ Platform B   │              │              │
│ Shared       │              │              │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** WHAT: only verifiable facts (cost, timeline, risk, adoption data) — one sticky per fact, tag A/B/Both. Challenge "A is better" → "What data supports that?" SO WHAT: interpretations allowed; no rebuttal during capture. NOW WHAT: open questions for criteria block. Cap each platform champion to 2 minutes total in WHAT — use a timer.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain three columns; silent capture 3 min on WHAT |
| 2 | 5 min | Harvest WHAT — facilitator reads stickies, no debate |
| 3 | 4 min | SO WHAT — round-robin one reaction each, scribe captures |
| 4 | 4 min | NOW WHAT — cluster open questions; star items for criteria |
| 5 | 2 min | Summarize: "Shared facts vs open questions" |

**Block output:** Three-column wall with tagged facts, meanings, and open questions.

### Block: Criteria workshop (0:37–0:52)

**Why:** Ranked preferences must be against **agreed criteria**, not positional bargaining — criteria-before-vote is non-negotiable.

**Format:** [ToP Focused Conversation](https://www.top-network.org/use-focused-conversation) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CRITERIA (draft → refine → weight)                          │
│  Criterion          │ Weight (dots) │ Notes                  │
│  ───────────────────┼───────────────┼────────────────────    │
│  e.g. Time to value │ ●●●           │                        │
│  e.g. Total cost    │ ●●●●          │                        │
│  e.g. Risk          │ ●●            │                        │
│  (≥5 criteria, weights sum normalized visually)              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Start from NOW WHAT questions. Silent brainstorm criteria (3 min), cluster, then dot-weight (5 dots per person across criteria). Drop criteria below 2 total dots unless CFO requests keep. Document final list on a clean sheet — this becomes the scoring rubric. No platform names on criteria stickies during brainstorm.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | REFLECT: "What must we measure to choose well?" silent write |
| 2 | 4 min | Cluster criteria; dedupe to 6–8 candidates |
| 3 | 4 min | Dot-weight criteria (5 dots/person) |
| 4 | 3 min | INTERPRET: Confirm top 5–7 weighted criteria on clean sheet |
| 5 | 2 min | DECIDE (for this block): Criteria locked unless CFO objects |

**Block output:** Weighted criteria rubric on wall (photo + typed copy).

---

### Block: Structured debate (0:52–1:05)

**Why:** Directors need equal airtime on tensions before scoring — Conversation Cafe slows advocacy and surfaces trade-offs for the record.

**Format:** [Conversation Cafe](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/conversation-cafe)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CAFE QUESTION (one round, 3× ~4 min)                        │
│  "When you weigh [top criterion] against [second criterion],  │
│   what trade-off worries you most for our Q3 bet?"           │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Capture: TENSION │ WHO RAISED │ IMPLICATION FOR SCORING     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** One cafe round only (time box). Use talking object; no cross-talk during speaker turn. Scribe captures tensions verbatim — these feed dissent notes later. CFO speaks in turn like any director during cafe; separate CFO decision comment to close block.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Explain cafe rules and question |
| 2 | 9 min | One round (~1 min × 8 + buffer); scribe captures tensions |
| 3 | 3 min | Name 3–4 recurring tensions on board; no resolution debate |

**Block output:** Tension list linked to criteria; raw notes for recommendation memo.

### Block: Rank and score (1:05–1:22)

**Why:** Convert criteria into a transparent ranked preference — individual scoring prevents loudest-voice wins while preserving dissent.

**Format:** [Buy a Feature](https://www.luma-institute.com/buy-a-feature/) + [Gradients of Agreement](https://workshopweaver.com/facilitation-methods/gradients-of-agreement)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SCORING (individual worksheets → aggregate)                 │
│  Criterion │ Wt │ Platform A │ Platform B │                  │
│  ──────────┼────┼────────────┼────────────│                  │
│  ...       │    │  1-5 each  │  1-5 each  │                  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  BUY A FEATURE (optional tie-break): $100 pretend budget     │
│  across A features vs B features                             │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  GRADIENTS (on group recommendation):                        │
│  5 Fully support │ 4 Support │ 3 Can live │ 2 Reserves │ 1 No│
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Each director scores A and B per criterion (1–5) on paper — **individual, not show of hands**. Scribe aggregates weighted totals on board. Declare leading platform from math, not rhetoric. Then gradients: "Given this ranking, I recommend ___" — each person holds up 1–5 fingers simultaneously. Capture names at levels 1–3 as dissent lines (not anonymous — attributed accountability). If split within 5%, run 3-minute Buy a Feature tie-break on top 3 criteria only.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Explain scoring sheet; silent individual scoring |
| 2 | 5 min | Collect sheets; scribe posts weighted totals |
| 3 | 3 min | Declare leading option; read totals aloud |
| 4 | 4 min | Gradients of Agreement on group recommendation |
| 5 | 3 min | Capture dissent quotes at levels 1–3 on board |

**Block output:** Scoring matrix, weighted winner, gradient tally, dissent notes.

### Block: Close and handoff (1:22–1:30)

**Why:** Connect room output to CFO decision path; cross-role requests surface what directors need to accept an override or support the recommendation.

**Format:** [What I Need From You](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-i-need-from-you) + plain facilitation structure

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  RECOMMENDATION MEMO (draft headings)                        │
│  • Criteria + weights                                        │
│  • Scores + ranked preference                                │
│  • Dissent / reservations                                    │
│  • Open questions → CFO                                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WHAT I NEED FROM YOU (CFO ↔ directors, 1 request each max)  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Scribe reads draft memo headings from the board. One round: each director states one need from CFO (or from peers) to support Q3 execution — negotiable, not a task list. CFO closes with: when decision lands, how dissent will be recorded, next checkpoint date.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Scribe reads memo draft structure from board |
| 2 | 4 min | What I Need From You — one request each, no debate |
| 3 | 2 min | CFO confirms decision timeline and communication path |

**Block output:** Memo outline on board; requests list; decision timeline.

## Cut list

If running long:

- **Cut:** Shorten Conversation Cafe to [Rounds](https://patterns.sociocracy30.org/rounds.html) (one sentence each on top tension only); drop Buy a Feature tie-break; limit What I Need From You to CFO + two volunteers only.
- **Protect:** Authority framing (Block 1), criteria workshop (Block 5), individual scoring + Gradients of Agreement (Block 7), recommendation memo structure (Block 8).

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Boardroom with U-shape or two rows facing wall; two flip charts (context + criteria/scoring); individual scoring worksheets (printed); timers; scribe laptop for memo draft.

**Pre-read / pre-work:** Send both platform one-pagers and latest cost/timeline data 48h ahead; ask each director to bring 3 facts (not opinions) about each platform. CFO pre-read includes explicit note: "Your override authority is acknowledged; we need your decision process after input."

**Facilitator role:** External or internal neutral facilitator (not a director with a platform horse in the race). Scribe captures board content; no participant facilitates their own preference.

**Parking lot:** Right-hand column on main board; revisit only in break or if blocking criteria — otherwise defer to memo appendix.

## Goal

Help a seven-person remote team reflect on a rough sprint in a psychologically safe way and leave with 2–3 concrete, owned improvement actions the team can actually control.

## Key outcomes

- Shared safety baseline: the group knows whether honest discussion is possible and what guardrails apply.
- Emotional and process themes surfaced without blame or personal attacks.
- Clustered improvement areas with clear team-owned vs external constraints.
- 2–3 prioritized actions with named owners, due dates, and a review checkpoint before the next retro.

| Time | Block | Format (catalogue practice) | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:08 | Arrive and safety | [Safety Check](https://www.funretrospectives.com/safety-check/) | Safety score + agreed ground rules |
| 0:08–0:20 | Emotional check-in | [Draw Your Feelings](https://www.funretrospectives.com/draw-your-feelings/) | Anonymous mood sketches + named themes |
| 0:20–0:45 | Inspect sprint | [Starfish](https://www.teamretro.com/retro-template/starfish-retrospective) | Clustered stickies across five arms |
| 0:45–0:55 | Prioritize | [Dot Voting - Starfish](https://retromat.org/en/?id=49) | Top 3–5 themes for action |
| 0:55–0:68 | Scope and commit | [Circles & Soup / Circle of Influence](https://retromat.org/en/?id=29) + [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24) | 2–3 owned actions with owners and dates |
| 0:68–0:75 | Close | [Round of Admiration](https://retromat.org/en/?id=76) | Appreciation + parking lot + next checkpoint |

### Block: Arrive and safety (0:00–0:08)

**Why:** Low trust means the team may withhold honest input unless safety is tested and named explicitly before any sprint inspection.

**Format:** [Safety Check](https://www.funretrospectives.com/safety-check/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SAFETY CHECK (1 = not safe … 5 = fully safe)               │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]   ← each person places one dot         │
│                                                             │
│  Ground rules (edit together):                              │
│  • Focus on systems and sprint events, not people           │
│  • No fixing or debating during capture                     │
│  • What is shared here stays in this room                   │
│  • Parking lot for interpersonal issues → manager/1:1       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Open with a calm, neutral tone. State purpose: learn from the sprint and pick a few experiments—not assign blame. If the average safety score is below 3, say so plainly: "We will stay at the level the room supports—more listening, less debate, shorter blocks." Do not push for confessions. If someone marks 1–2, acknowledge without asking them to explain in plenary; offer a private follow-up. Read ground rules; ask for a thumb-up or chat "+1" to adopt. Avoid jokes about the rough sprint.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Welcome; state goal and 75-minute arc; remind cameras optional |
| 2 | 3 min | Explain Safety Check scale; each person adds one anonymous dot on the board |
| 3 | 2 min | Read approximate cluster; co-create ground rules on the board |
| 4 | 1 min | Confirm parking lot column exists for off-topic or interpersonal items |

**Block output:** Safety score snapshot and agreed ground rules captured on the live board.

### Block: Emotional check-in (0:08–0:20)

**Why:** After a rough sprint, feelings often block productive inspection; drawing lowers the cost of honesty and gives the facilitator a read on emotional temperature.

**Format:** [Draw Your Feelings](https://www.funretrospectives.com/draw-your-feelings/)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DRAW YOUR FEELINGS — sprint as a picture (no words required) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  [anon frame 1] [anon frame 2] [anon frame 3] …             │
│                                                             │
│  Optional label (if you want): one word under your drawing  │
│  Theme cluster → sticky notes beside grouped frames         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Emphasize anonymity: use board frames with no names, or sticky notes placed without attribution. No interpretation of others' drawings. Invite optional one-word labels only—no explanations unless volunteered. If energy is flat, shorten sharing to silent gallery walk (everyone scans the board, adds one "I notice…" sticky). Do not ask "who drew this?"

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Explain prompt: "Draw how this sprint felt—abstract, metaphor, weather, anything" |
| 2 | 5 min | Silent drawing in personal frames or anonymous stickies |
| 3 | 3 min | Gallery scan; each person adds max one "I notice…" observation sticky |
| 4 | 3 min | Facilitator clusters observations into 3–5 theme labels (exhaustion, confusion, pressure…) |

**Block output:** Anonymous drawings plus clustered emotional themes on the board; themes inform Starfish prompts but are not debated.

### Block: Inspect sprint (0:20–0:45)

**Why:** Starfish gives nuance beyond start/stop/continue—important when the sprint was messy and practices need tuning, not just blame.

**Format:** [Starfish](https://www.teamretro.com/retro-template/starfish-retrospective)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
                    ┌──────────────┐
                    │  KEEP DOING  │
         ┌──────────┤              ├──────────┐
         │ MORE OF  │              │ LESS OF  │
         │   THIS   │              │   THIS   │
         └──────────┤              ├──────────┘
                    │  START DOING │
                    │  STOP DOING  │
                    └──────────────┘
        Silent write → read aloud in rounds → cluster duplicates
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Silent brainwrite first—critical for low trust. Use rounds (chat or voice) so every voice is heard once before open discussion. Cluster stickies; do not resolve debates—capture tension in the parking lot. Redirect personal criticism to system language: "What happened in the workflow?" not "Who failed?" Timebox each arm if the group stalls.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 min | Introduce five arms with one example each; point to emotional themes as prompts |
| 2 | 10 min | Silent capture—one sticky per idea, all arms |
| 3 | 8 min | Round-robin read: each person reads one sticky per pass until done or time ends |
| 4 | 5 min | Cluster duplicates; facilitator names 8–12 visible theme groups |

**Block output:** Populated Starfish board with clustered themes ready for voting.

### Block: Prioritize (0:45–0:55)

**Why:** Seven people can generate more issues than the team can act on; dot voting makes focus transparent and democratic.

**Format:** [Dot Voting - Starfish](https://retromat.org/en/?id=49)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  CLUSTERS (from Starfish)          dots: 3 per person       │
│  [Theme A] ●●●●        [Theme B] ●●                          │
│  [Theme C] ●●●●●●      [Theme D] ●                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Three dots per person; may stack on one cluster. No lobbying during voting. After votes, select top 3–5 clusters for action work—not every high-vote item must become an action; facilitator confirms with the group: "These are our focus areas for the next 13 minutes."

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 min | Explain rules: 3 dots, vote on clusters not individuals |
| 2 | 3 min | Silent voting on board |
| 3 | 4 min | Read top clusters; group confirms focus list |
| 4 | 2 min | Move lower-vote items to parking lot or "watch" column |

**Block output:** Ranked list of 3–5 focus themes with vote counts visible on the board.

### Block: Scope and commit (0:55–0:68)

**Why:** Low-trust teams often propose actions outside their control, then disengage when nothing changes; separating circle of influence from "soup" keeps commitments credible.

**Format:** [Circles & Soup / Circle of Influence](https://retromat.org/en/?id=29) + [Open Items List](https://retromat.org/en/?id=24)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┐
│  TEAM CAN CHANGE         │  SOUP (escalate / accept / park) │
│  (Circle of influence)   │                                  │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│  Action │ Owner │ Due    │  Item │ Escalate to │ Status     │
│  ───────┼───────┼──────   │  ─────┼─────────────┼──────      │
│         │       │        │       │             │            │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┘
        Target: 2–3 actions max — specific, doable this sprint
```

**Facilitator instruction:** For each top theme, ask: "What can *we* change without waiting for others?" Capture soup items separately—do not let them become vague team actions. Each action needs one owner (volunteer, not assigned by facilitator unless asked), a due date before next retro, and a one-line success signal. Push back on "communicate better"—ask for a observable behavior. Limit to 2–3 actions even if the group wants more.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 min | Take top theme #1: brainstorm team-owned moves; park soup items |
| 2 | 4 min | Repeat for themes #2 and #3 (or split pairs if time tight) |
| 3 | 3 min | Draft Open Items List: action, owner, due date, success signal |
| 4 | 2 min | Each owner reads their action aloud and confirms commitment |

**Block output:** Open Items List with 2–3 team-owned actions; soup/escalation list with named escalation path where applicable.

### Block: Close (0:68–0:75)

**Why:** Ending with appreciation rebuilds relational safety after difficult content; explicit next steps prevent retro actions from dying in chat.

**Format:** [Round of Admiration](https://retromat.org/en/?id=76)

**Board / frame (ASCII):**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  APPRECIATION (one sentence each, no debate)                │
│  → person or "the team" for something this sprint           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  NEXT: summary posted today │ review at standup + next retro │
│  Parking lot → [link / owner for follow-up]                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Facilitator instruction:** Keep admiration brief and sincere—no forced positivity. Summarize actions from the Open Items List. State post-session timeline aloud. Thank the group for showing up honestly given low trust.

**Timed flow:**

| Step | Time | Facilitator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 min | Round: each person shares one appreciation (pass allowed) |
| 2 | 2 min | Recap 2–3 actions, owners, dates; mention summary and review date |
| 3 | 1 min | Close meeting; stop recording if applicable |

**Block output:** Verbal commitments confirmed; parking lot location assigned; participants know when summary arrives.

## Preparation

**Space / tools:** Video call with gallery view (Zoom, Meet, or Teams); shared digital board as single source of truth (Miro, FigJam, EasyRetro, or TeamRetro); timer visible to facilitator; anonymous sticky capability or pre-built unnamed frames for Draw Your Feelings; dot-voting feature or emoji dots; scribe with edit access; backup board link in calendar invite.

**Pre-read / pre-work:** Send 24 hours ahead: (1) retro link and board link, (2) one sentence on purpose—"reflect on the sprint safely and pick 2–3 experiments," (3) optional async prompt: "One thing that helped / one thing that hurt this sprint" as private prep (not required), (4) note that interpersonal conflicts go to parking lot or 1:1, not plenary debate. Ask manager/sponsor not to attend unless the team explicitly wants them—presence can suppress honesty.

**Facilitator role:** Lead facilitator runs safety, timeboxing, and redirecting blame; scribe captures actions in real time on the board; optional co-facilitator monitors chat, raised hands, and reads chat contributions aloud so quiet voices are included.

**Parking lot:** Dedicated board column for interpersonal issues, soup/escalation items, and deferred themes. Facilitator reviews parking lot within 48 hours with team lead; interpersonal items route to private conversations—not group diagnosis in this session.

---

## Hybrid / online parity

**Digital board:** One shared board link opened before start; all capture, voting, and action tables live there—not in slide decks or facilitator-only notes.

**Online group:** All seven participants are remote; no room-side advantage. Use round-robin with chat-first option: "Type in chat, then I'll call on volunteers." Breakouts not needed at this size; plenary with silent write scales better for low trust.

**Parity rules:**
- If it is not on the board, it did not happen—actions, ground rules, and parking lot included.
- Silent writing before discussion on every main block.
- Voting only on the board—no show-of-hands in a hypothetical room.
- Online speakers first in read-aloud rounds; facilitator reads chat entries verbatim when someone prefers not to speak.
- Cameras optional; do not comment on camera status.

**Co-facilitation:** Scribe watches board hygiene (clustering, action table). If no co-facilitator, lead facilitator pauses capture every 10 minutes to catch up stickies and confirm chat was read.

## Post-session process

| Step | Owner | When | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Publish summary + action table with board link | Scribe | Same day (within 4 hours) | Tag action owners in team channel |
| Create tasks/tickets for each action | Action owners | Within 24 hours | Link tasks to retro summary |
| Review parking lot and soup escalations | Team lead + facilitator | Within 2 business days | Close loop or schedule targeted follow-up |
| Check action progress | Facilitator or Scrum Master | Daily standup, first 3 days | Unblock or resize stuck actions |
| Revisit outcomes and safety | Whole team | Next retro (~2 weeks) | Close or replace actions; repeat Safety Check to measure shift |
